Balanced factorial effect analysis
================================================================================

This implementation does not use statsmodels.
Sums of squares are calculated directly from balanced factorial marginal means.

Effect ratios
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
pH signal ratio = SS_pH / corrected total SS
nuisance-main ratio = (SS_phone + SS_light + SS_phone:light) / total SS
pH-dependent interaction ratio = (SS_pH:phone + SS_pH:light) / total SS
